Our Founder

Built on two decades
of material knowledge.

Karan Sethi didn't arrive at architecture through a textbook. He arrived through furniture — importing, sourcing, and selling it from 2004 across multiple Delhi retail locations. That background gave him something most designers don't have: an understanding of how things are actually made, what quality feels like before it's installed, and which materials perform over time versus which ones photograph well.

When he transitioned into interiors in 2011, that material intelligence became the foundation of the studio's design process. By 2016, the retail stores closed and Qube became a full-service design practice. The instinct remained: start with what lasts.

"We build more than structures — we design customised experiences tuned to your aspirations."
2004
