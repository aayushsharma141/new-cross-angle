# ADR-003: Architectural Grotesque & Classical Serif Typography

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Deciders:** Art Director, Lead Product Designer  

## Context
The previous font pairing relied on standard Google Serif and geometric DM Sans, which felt round and consumer-tech oriented.

## Decision
- **Display Headlines:** *Cormorant Garamond* (Classical Serif) with tight character tracking (`-0.03em`) and custom line-height (`1.1`).
- **Body & Structural Copy:** *Instrument Sans* / *Inter* (Grotesque) with neutral tracking (`0em`) and line-height (`1.6`).
- **Labels & Metadata:** Uppercase small caps with wide tracking (`+0.18em`) to simulate chiseled architectural inscriptions.

## Consequences
- Elevates editorial elegance and readability.
- Replaces generic SaaS font defaults.
