SECTION 7 — AEO: ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION 
📌 What is AEO? 
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of optimizing content to 
appear in direct answer features: Featured Snippets, People Also Ask (PAA), 
Google's AI Overviews (SGE), voice search results, and zero-click answers. 
AEO is the bridge between traditional SEO and AI-powered search. 
 
7.1  Featured Snippet Optimization 
☐ Identify questions your target keywords trigger ('who', 'what', 'how', 'when', 'why') 
☐ Structure answers in the first 40–60 words after the question heading 
☐ Use definition boxes for 'What is...' queries (40–60 word clear definition) 
☐ Use numbered lists for 'How to...' queries (steps format) 
☐ Use bullet lists for 'Best ways to...' or comparison queries 
☐ Use tables for comparison, pricing, or specification queries 
☐ Place target question as an H2 or H3 heading exactly as users search it

☐ Mark up FAQ content with FAQ Schema / Q&A Schema

7.3  Google AI Overviews (SGE) Optimization

SECTION 9 — AI SEO STRATEGY 
📌 AI SEO: Optimizing for the AI-Powered Search Era 
AI SEO refers to strategies that help your content rank in, and be cited by, 
AI-powered search engines and assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, 
Claude, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Apple Intelligence). 
The goal: become a trusted, cited source inside AI-generated answers. 
 
9.1  Entity SEO & Knowledge Graph Optimization 
☐ Define your brand as an entity: consistent Name, Address, Phone (NAP) across all platforms 
☐ Create / claim your Google Knowledge Panel 
☐ Create a Wikipedia / Wikidata entry if brand meets notability guidelines 
☐ Use Organization schema with sameAs properties linking to all social profiles 
☐ Earn mentions on authoritative, well-indexed websites 
☐ Build a consistent brand identity: logo, About Us, team pages, and mission statement 
 
9.2  Optimizing for AI Citation & LLM Visibility 
☐ Publish well-cited, factual, expert-authored content (LLMs cite authoritative sources) 
☐ Create structured, clearly formatted content — AI models prefer scannable formats 
☐ Use direct, factual language in introductions — avoid vague or hedging openers 
☐ Create 'definition' content: 'What is [X]?' pages for your core industry terms 
☐ Aim to be cited in industry directories, Wikipedia, and authoritative roundups 
☐ Ensure your site is accessible with no crawl blocks that prevent AI bot access 
☐ Build topical authority in a specific niche rather than covering too many topics 
☐ Create data pages and original research — LLMs actively cite unique statistics 
 
9.3  AI-Assisted Content Creation — Best Practices 
☐ Use AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) for research, outlines and ideation — not final output 
☐ Always add human expertise, original insight and first-hand experience to AI drafts 
☐ Fact-check all AI-generated content against authoritative sources 
☐ Ensure final content passes E-E-A-T standards — AI alone cannot provide true expertise 
☐ Do not publish pure AI-generated content — Google targets 'AI-first' content specifically 
☐ Use AI tools for: internal linking suggestions, meta tag optimization, content gap analysis
