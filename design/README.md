### Knowledge Domain Separation
- **Playbook:** Active production decisions and rules.
- **R&D:** Historical and external discoveries.
- **ADR:** Documented intentional exceptions.
- **Reviews:** Empirical audit evidence.
- **Tokens:** Quantitative engineering contracts.
