# Project Narrative Template

**Purpose:** This template governs the structure of every project featured in the Crossangle Interior portfolio.
The Project Detail page is not a feature list, a generic gallery, or a marketing pitch. It is a **Transformation Story**.

Every section exists to answer **one specific question** for the user. If content does not answer that question, it must be removed.

---

## 1. Arrival

- **User Question:** What am I about to experience?
- **Content:** Full-bleed, monumental hero image of the project's most defining outcome. A singular, bold title. Minimal meta-data (location, year).
- **Tone:** Silent, anticipatory, focused.

## 2. Context (The Problem)

- **User Question:** Why did this project exist?
- **Content:** The origin of the project. The client’s initial reality. The structural or aesthetic failing of the original space.
- **Format:** Brief, prose-driven narrative text.

## 3. Constraints

- **User Question:** What made it difficult?
- **Content:** Budget limitations, impossible timelines, load-bearing walls, zoning laws, or conflicting stakeholder desires.
- **Format:** A focused description of the friction that forced creative problem-solving.

## 4. Design Thinking

- **User Question:** Why were these decisions made?
- **Content:** The breakthrough idea. How the constraints were turned into features. The conceptual anchor of the project.
- **Format:** Text accompanied by conceptual sketches, floor plans, or mood boards.

## 5. Material Decisions

- **User Question:** Why these materials?
- **Content:** A hyper-focused look at the tactile choices. Why brushed brass instead of polished? Why walnut instead of oak?
- **Format:** Macro-photography of textures, paired with brief rationale.

## 6. Construction / Process

- **User Question:** How did it become real?
- **Content:** The messy middle. A glimpse into the execution.
- **Format:** "Behind the scenes" photography, showing craftsmanship, precision, and the reality of the work.

## 7. Transformation

- **User Question:** What changed?
- **Content:** The direct comparison between the `Context` and the `Outcome`.
- **Format:** Side-by-side or slider comparisons (Before/After), focusing on spatial or lighting shifts.

## 8. Outcome

- **User Question:** Did it succeed?
- **Content:** The final result, focusing on how the space is lived in, not just how it looks empty.
- **Format:** The main editorial photography sequence. High-rhythm (Image → Silence → Caption).

## 9. Reflection

- **User Question:** What should I remember?
- **Content:** A singular takeaway. What did Crossangle learn from this? What makes this project unforgettable?
- **Format:** A pull quote from the Lead Designer or Client.

## 10. Next Project

- **User Question:** Where do I go now?
- **Content:** The bridge to the next journey.
- **Format:** A single, compelling image and title linking directly to another Transformation Story.

---

**Core Rule:** Do not use generic feature lists, "Services Used" tags, or empty marketing copy. Speak to the process, the friction, and the resolution.
